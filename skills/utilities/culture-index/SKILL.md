# Interpreting Culture Index

Interprets Culture Index (CI) psychometric profiles for individual analysis, team composition, burnout detection, and hiring profile matching.

## When to Use

- Understanding an individual's Culture Index profile
- Analyzing team composition and complementarity
- Identifying burnout risk signals
- Matching candidates to role requirements
- Planning team restructuring or role assignments

## Core Traits

Culture Index measures seven traits:

| Trait | Symbol | High = | Low = |
|-------|--------|--------|-------|
| Autonomy | A | Independent, self-directed | Collaborative, team-oriented |
| Social Ability | B | People-focused, persuasive | Task-focused, reserved |
| Pace | C | Patient, consistent | Urgent, fast-paced |
| Conformity | D | Rule-following, detail-oriented | Flexible, unconventional |
| Logic | E | Analytical, abstract | Practical, concrete |
| Energy | N | High-energy, intense | Steady, conservative |
| Stability | I | Volatile, reactive | Stable, composed |

## 18 Archetypes

Culture Index identifies 18 archetypes based on trait combinations. Key archetypes:

- **Craftsman**: High D, Low B — technical excellence, detail-oriented
- **Scholar**: High E, High D — analytical, research-oriented
- **Rainmaker**: High A, High B — entrepreneurial, relationship-driven
- **Trailblazer**: High A, Low C — visionary, fast-moving
- **Administrator**: High D, Low A — process-oriented, organized
- **Coordinator**: Balanced — bridge-builder, consensus-seeker

See [references/](references/) for detailed descriptions of all 18 archetypes.
